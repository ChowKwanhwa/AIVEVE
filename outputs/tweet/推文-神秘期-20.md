## C. AI / Industry 深度观察(5 条 — thinker 人设,不提 Aivive)

```
C1:

the hardest part of building an AI product in 2026
isn't the model.
isn't the prompt.
isn't even the harness.

it's deciding what your users do *with* the output.
```

```
C2:

chatboxes were the calculator era of AI products.

the spreadsheet hasn't shipped yet.
```

```
C3:

generation cost dropped 100x in eighteen months.

attention cost didn't drop at all.

we know which one matters.
```

```
C4:

"AI agent" was a useful word in 2024.

in 2026 it's a tax.
nobody says it anymore — they describe what the thing does instead.

a good sign.
```

```
C5:

three things solved AI products in 2025:
context length, tool use, structured output.

three things didn't:
taste, memory, social context.

guess which three the next cycle is about.
```
