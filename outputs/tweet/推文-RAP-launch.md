## A. RAP 品类宣称(4 条 — 把"我们开创新品类"打出去)

```
A1 — pinnable category claim:

Today we're claiming a new category: Recursive AI Protocol.

An RAP is an on-chain economic system where each act of
using the AI product recursively reduces the supply of its
native asset. Automatically. Verifiably. Without intermediation.

Aivive is the first.
```

```
A2 — the loop in 1 line:

Recursive AI Protocol (RAP).

  use → revenue → supply ↓ → value ↑ → more use

The recursion is the entire point. Every turn of the loop
reinforces the next, with no human in the path.

Whitepaper ↓
```

```
A3 — RAP vs "AI + token":

Most "AI x Crypto" projects are AI products with a token bolted on.

A Recursive AI Protocol is the inverse: the token economy is
the design, the AI product is the input.

Different category. Different math. Different outcome.
```

```
A4 — Web2 vs Web3 framing:

If subscriptions are how Web2 AI products fund themselves,
and middlemen extract the surplus —

RAPs are how Web3 AI products fund themselves, and the
surplus compresses the float.

Same revenue. Different destination.
```

## C. Compounding / Monetary Policy(3 条 — 升级版叙事)

```
C1 — 复利 anchor:

Compounding is the most underrated word in crypto.

Every weekly burn cycle on Aivive is a compounding event.
Tokens get scarcer. Per-token weight goes up. The next
user's contribution carries more weight than the last.

Math beats marketing.
```

```
C2 — manifesto 短句:

The act of using Aivive is, by construction,
an act of monetary policy.

You don't need to read this twice —
but you should think about it twice.
```

```
C3 — 引入 velocity 概念:

Most token economies pay attention to two variables:
supply and demand.

A Recursive AI Protocol pays attention to a third:
usage velocity.

Velocity is what links the AI product surface to the
monetary surface. It's what makes the recursion possible.
```
