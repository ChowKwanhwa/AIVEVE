## 🇺🇸 English (主版本)

**Aivive** is a deflationary AI-native economy spanning Solana and Base. Every dollar of revenue from its AI generation tools is automatically bridged via Circle's CCTP, swapped on Solana through Jupiter, and permanently burned on-chain — turning consumer AI activity into verifiable token scarcity. There's no token to stake, no gating to use the product, and no human in the loop after deployment. Just a self-funded, fully auditable feedback cycle where every burn is a public Solscan transaction.

**aivive.ai** is the flagship AI image generation feed running on Aivive. It's where AI-generated visual content meets social discovery for creators, designers, and the curious. Creators build prompt-backed profiles and share work in a Civitai-meets-Pinterest feed where remix is one click and taste lives in the UI, not the chatbox. Reputation is earned through what you ship, while every generation quietly fuels the burn loop running in the background.
